# 8. Market Day

> **READ-ONLY REFERENCE.** Do not edit this file during feature work. If the code disagrees with it, stop and ask the developer. Change it only when the developer explicitly asks. Verified against `farmers-market-platform-web` (working tree based on `fb09982`) and `farmers-market-platform` (working tree based on `ea2c77a`), November 27, 2026.

**Layer: Structure, then Styling, in separate parts.**

Market Day replaces the homepage while a market is open. It is shared by every market theme and borrows the theme's hero.

## Part A: Structure

### When it shows

- `pages/index.astro` asks for the Market Day state only when `marketDayLandingAllowed` is true: the path is `/`, the storefront is a backend market, the page is not in the editor preview, and the URL does not carry `?storefront=regular`.
- If any market date is open, the page renders `components/market/MarketDayHome.astro` instead of the theme's `MarketHome`. With more than one open, `?marketDay=<id>` picks one; without it the shopper chooses.
- When no date is open, the page renders the normal homepage plus the `MarketDayLive` island, which switches to Market Day when a date opens.
- `/?storefront=regular` always shows the normal homepage. The Market Day hero links to it ("Shop ahead for another date").
- Market Day is never shown in the editor preview, so it cannot be edited there.

### What it contains

1. **The theme hero, with fixed copy.** `MarketDayHome` looks the hero up in `themeHeroes` (`meadow`, `hearth`, `pasture`, each with its prefix) and renders it with `override`: the title "Welcome to <market name>", a short introduction, a primary button "Find a booth" (to `#market-day-map`) with the right arrow after the label, and a secondary button "Today's vendors" (to `#market-day-vendors`). With several open dates the primary button is "Choose your market" and there is no secondary button.
2. **Hero details slot:** the `MarketDayCountdown` island, the venue line (map pin icon and venue), and the "Shop ahead for another date" link, which carries the class `<prefix>-more market-day-regular`.
3. **The live map and pickups:** `MarketDayLive` with the published booth map.
4. **Vendors today:** `MarketVendorCards` with `marketDay` and, on themes with `marketDayBoothCards`, booth details.
5. **Choosing a date** (when several are open): a list of open dates, each linking to `/?marketDay=<id>`.

The wrapper is `<div class="market-day-home <prefix>-home <prefix>-day" data-composition="market" data-template="<theme>">`. A theme's Market Day styles hang off `.<prefix>-day`.

### Booth cards (rule `marketDayBoothCards`)

- Pointing at a booth on the map shows that vendor's card, built from the same classes as the Vendors today cards: the booth name first, then the vendor's picture (or initial) with the vendor's name and main category, then a **Visit storefront** link. The vendor's name is plain text, not a link. There is no booth size and no "Assigned to..." line.
- The card stays open while the pointer is on it and closes shortly after it leaves. A booth with no vendor shows its name and "Open booth".
- `MarketMap` has an opt-in `renderHoverCard` prop. Without it, the map keeps its read-only details tooltip, which the manager's tools still use.
- The public map may omit a vendor's id, so the card finds today's vendor by id first, then by name (`lib/market-day-booths.ts`). Category codes such as `FRESH_PRODUCE` read "Fresh produce".
- **Vendors today** cards show the vendor's main category and a link "Find their booth (A01) →". The whole card is that link. It scrolls to the map, moves to that booth, selects it and opens its card. A vendor with no booth shows "Not on today's map" and is not a link. `MarketMap` takes a `locateId` and calls `onLocated` once done.

### The Vendors page during Market Day (every theme)

While a market is in Market Day mode, `/vendors` defaults to that date until it ends (a requested `?date=` still wins). Its cards link to `/?marketDay=<id>&booth=<elementId>#market-day-map`, which opens the booth on the homepage map. The summary link reads "See today's market map" instead of linking to the shop, which no longer sells a date that has started. Only themes with `marketDayBoothCards` open a card on arrival.

### What a new theme must provide

- An entry in `themeHeroes` in `MarketDayHome.astro` with its `Hero` and prefix. (Market Harvest and market Editorial Market are older and have their own branches in `MarketDayHome`: Harvest uses `HarvestHero`, Editorial its own hero artwork. Their `marketDayBoothCards` rule is off until each styles the booth card.)
- A `Hero.astro` that supports `override` and the `details` slot (see [Structure contract](03_STRUCTURE_CONTRACT.md)).
- Styles for `.<prefix>-more` (used by the "Shop ahead" link) and for `.<prefix>-day` content.

## Part B: Styling

### Rules

- **Keep the everyday hero.** Market Day never recolors the hero background, the title treatment, the text or the buttons. The only additions are a small "Market day" tag above the title and the countdown below the buttons.
- **The hero slides under the header** exactly as the normal homepage hero does.
- **Style the countdown as part of the theme**, not as a plain card. A theme with a signature title treatment reuses it here.
- **The countdown is only as wide as its contents** (the clock and the opening-hours line), never stretched across the column.
- **The venue line uses the theme's heading color.**
- **Booth cards share the Vendors today card look**, a little smaller, with the same eased tilt on the picture. The card fades in. The booth name sits in its top-left corner, with the picture beside the vendor's name and category.
- **The map and the pickup panels stay on light surfaces** so they stay readable.

### Hooks

`.<prefix>-day .<prefix>-hero-inner`, `.market-day-hero-details`, `.market-day-venue`, `.market-day-countdown` and its parts (`.market-day-countdown-label`, `.market-day-countdown-hours`, `.market-day-clock-lead`, `.market-day-unit`, `.market-day-countdown-note`, `.market-day-digit`, `.market-day-separator`, `.market-day-live`, `.market-day-progress`), `.market-day-regular`, `.harvest-section`, `.market-day-pickups-section`, `.market-day-choices`, and for booth cards `.booth-hover-card.booth-hover-card-custom`, `.market-day-booth-card`, `.market-day-booth-name`, `.market-day-booth-vendor`, `.market-day-booth-text`.

### Pasture's choices (example)

- A red pill tag with a pulsing white dot reads "Market day" above the title.
- The countdown is painted on the same wooden sign as the title: green slats, a nail in each corner, slat ends sticking out of both sides and white lettering. The digits sit on cream plates. The progress bar is a sunflower stripe in a dark green groove.
- Vendors today and the date choices are cream cards that lift on hover. Pickups sit on a sunflower-soft panel.
