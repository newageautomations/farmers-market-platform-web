# 6. Checkout

> **READ-ONLY REFERENCE.** Do not edit this file during feature work. If the code disagrees with it, stop and ask the developer. Change it only when the developer explicitly asks. Verified against `farmers-market-platform-web` `b8503ea` and `farmers-market-platform` `15dd932`, November 26, 2026.

**Layer: Structure, then Styling, in separate parts.** The flow, the components and the rules are shared by every market theme and are fixed. A theme only restyles them.

This file covers market storefronts. Vendor storefronts have their own cart and checkout (`VendorCart`, `VendorCheckout`); notes about those (for example `docs/STOREFRONT_CHECKOUT_UI.md`) do not apply here.

## Part A: Structure (fixed for every theme)

### Authority

The backend owns prices, fees, taxes, stock, date eligibility, pickup options, vendor terms and payment state. The storefront only displays what the backend returns and sends the shopper's choices back. A theme never computes a total, hides a fee, skips a step or changes a message about money or payment.

### The whole flow

```
Shop (/shop)                      choose a market date, add products
  │  market date picker            ShopMarketSelection + MarketPickupCalendar in a Dialog
  │  Add to cart                   market-cart-button on ProductCards, purchase form on product detail
  ▼
Cart (/cart)                      MarketCart island: one group per vendor
  ▼  Continue to checkout
Checkout (/checkout)              Checkout island with marketLayout
  1. Contact                      name, email, optional phone
  2. Pickup                       one pickup choice per vendor
  3. Review & Pay                 summary, vendor terms, Stripe card entry
  4. Confirmation                 committed order → redirect
  ▼
Confirmation (/checkout/success?order=<id>)   PurchaseSuccess island
```

### 1. Choosing a market date

- The shop sells for one market date at a time. The date comes from `?occurrence=<id>`, the saved choice cookie or the cart's date. Without one, `/shop` shows a "choose a date" state and no catalog.
- The date banner (`VendorPreorderBanner`) and picker (`ShopMarketSelection` with `MarketPickupCalendar` inside the shared `Dialog`) are shared. A cart that already has items is protected from switching to another date.
- On the Vendors page the same picker runs in browse-only mode: it navigates to `/vendors?date=<id>` and never changes the shopper's pickup date.

### 2. Adding to cart

- Product cards and the product detail purchase form call the backend through the shared purchase controller (`useProductPurchase`). The backend decides whether the add succeeds.
- A rejected add re-reads that product and says "This item is out of stock." or "Only N left in stock" (`lib/add-failure-message.ts`), otherwise the shared cart message. When the platform requires Stripe for sales, the backend also refuses to add an online order line for a vendor whose Stripe setup is not ready (`VendorSalesEligibility.require` in the backend's `vendor-sales-eligibility.service.ts`). The storefront shows "This vendor cannot accept online orders right now." (`VENDOR_STRIPE_SETUP_REQUIRED`).
- A successful add fires `market-cart-updated`, which updates the header cart count and bumps the cart button (`[data-bump]`).

### 3. Cart (`/cart`)

- `pages/cart.astro` reads the market cart on the server and renders `MarketCart` (`client:load`).
- The cart shows the chosen date banner, **Your Cart**, one group per vendor (vendor name heading, lines with image, quantity steppers and Remove), **Continue Shopping**, and an **Order Summary** with totals, loyalty (`CheckoutLoyalty`) and **Continue to checkout** (`/checkout`).
- An empty cart shows "Your cart is empty".
- In the editor preview the cart is not rendered ("Cart preview is not available in the visual editor.").

### 4. Checkout (`/checkout`)

`pages/checkout/index.astro` reads the market cart, the checkout status (`readCheckoutStatus`), the market date and line images on the server, then renders the shared `Checkout` island with `marketLayout` and, on market-only themes, `concise`. Stripe's return parameters on the URL are removed with a 303 redirect. The page is `private, no-store` and `noindex, nofollow`.

The layout has a heading, the chosen date banner (before Review), a progress list, the current stage and an order summary.

- **Progress** (`checkoutSteps`): Contact, Pickup, Review & Pay, Confirmation, in a `<nav aria-label="Checkout progress">` list. The current step has `aria-current="step"`. Completed steps are buttons back to that step until the order is committed. Later steps cannot be skipped to. A visually hidden heading takes focus on every stage change.
- **Back** returns to the previous stage. Each stage keeps what was entered.
- **Loyalty** (`CheckoutLoyalty`) appears for signed-in shoppers before Review. On markets it shows earned rewards only.
- **Order summary** (before Review): a shared `Dropdown`, open by default, titled "Order summary" (or "Order summary before fees" while pickup fees are still unknown), showing the total or "Updating…", the lines and an **Edit cart** link.

**Stage 1, Contact.** First name, last name and email are required. Email is read-only when the shopper is signed in. Phone is optional. No account is needed. Errors appear on blur or on submit, next to their fields. **Continue to pickup**.

**Stage 2, Pickup.**

- `CheckoutPickup` shows the market date, the time range, the venue, any pickup instructions and a map link for the venue.
- One fieldset per vendor (legend: vendor name) lists that vendor's pickup options as radio choices: name, fee when not zero, and instructions. A single option is chosen for the shopper.
- A vendor with no pickup option shows "Pickup is currently unavailable for this Vendor." and **Review order** stays disabled.
- If an option changed while the shopper was choosing, the options are re-read and the stale choice is dropped.

**Stage 3, Review & Pay.**

- The shopper's contact details, the pickup summary with each vendor's chosen option, and the review summary column (`CheckoutReviewSummary`) with lines, images and totals.
- When payments run through Stripe (`availability.mode === 'STRIPE_TEST'` in this environment), `StripeCheckout` loads once the preorder fee is known ("Updating order total…" until then):
  - The card form is Stripe's Payment Element in an accordion layout, created with the shopper's name, email and phone already filled.
  - With more than one vendor, the shopper is told the total appears as separate vendor charges, and each vendor's charge is listed.
  - `CheckoutTermsAcceptance` shows the vendors' terms. Paying needs the terms accepted. If a vendor's terms change, acceptance resets and the checkout refreshes.
  - Status is polled every 4 seconds. A processing `Dialog` with a `Spinner` announces authorizing and completing. Declines, failures, cancellations, refunds and uncertain outcomes each have their own message, and the shopper is told never to pay twice.
- Without Stripe the stage shows the local test payment (in local development) or "Online payment is not currently configured. Your order has not been placed."
- An interrupted card setup or a cancelled payment rebuilds the cart through the Shop APIs (`lib/restart-checkout.ts`), using an encrypted recovery cookie so quantities are never doubled.

**Stage 4, Confirmation.** Once the backend reports the order committed, the island links or redirects to the confirmation page.

### 5. Confirmation (`/checkout/success?order=<id>`)

- The route asks the backend for that order's status. A query-string id alone discloses nothing: the backend checks the order belongs to the session.
- A committed order renders `PurchaseSuccess`: "Thank you for your order", the items with images, totals, contact details, pickup details, a free-account invitation (signup prefilled from checkout, phone verification still required), a link to the shopper's orders and **Continue shopping** (`/shop`).
- A failed or still-pending payment says the purchase has not been confirmed, with **Check checkout status**.

### The `concise` flag

Market-only themes pass `concise` to the date banner, cart, checkout and Stripe form. It removes general guidance only:

- **Removed:** how preordering works, "Market hours:" prefixes, "Enter your pickup contact. You can purchase without an account.", "Payment details are securely handled by Stripe." and similar reassurance.
- **Always kept:** the market's own pickup instructions, vendor charge disclosures, the purchase authorization line, statuses, warnings and errors.

A new market-only theme gets `concise` automatically through `marketOnlyTheme`. Do not add a theme check to turn it on.

### What the editor preview does with commerce

Cart, checkout and confirmation never render in the editor preview. Add to cart buttons keep their look but do nothing. The preview bridge blocks every form submit and button action.

## Part B: Styling (what a theme may change)

### Rules

- Style the shared classes only, scoped to the site class. Do not wrap, reorder or rebuild the checkout markup.
- **The Stripe card fields are Stripe's.** They render in Stripe's own frame with Stripe's default appearance; the theme cannot and must not try to style inside it. Style only the container, charges list and messages around it.
- Never hide a fee row, disclosure, authorization line, status or error. The `concise` flag already removed what may be removed.
- Keep all controls at least 44px tall on touch screens and keep visible focus.
- Label and value rows in totals (`.checkout-totals dd`, `.vendor-cart-summary dd`) get `margin: 0; align-self: center;` and are checked for a 0px center offset.
- Lifted rows (progress buttons, pickup choices) drop their motion under reduced motion.

### Pasture's choices (example)

- Progress steps are numbered chips (a CSS counter in a small badge). The current step is filled with the leading color, with a sunflower badge and the button underside. Completed steps lift on hover and press on click.
- Each stage, and each vendor's pickup fieldset, is a cream card with the theme edge and stack shadow. Fieldset legends are headings over a dotted rule.
- Pickup choices are white rows that lift on hover. The chosen one turns sunflower-soft with an inset bar in the leading color.
- The order summary is a cream card. Each line's quantity is its own small chip beside the name.
- Continue buttons share the Back button's height; only their width differs.
