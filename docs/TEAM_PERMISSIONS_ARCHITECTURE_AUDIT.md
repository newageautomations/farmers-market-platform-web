# Team and permissions architecture audit

October 31, 2026. Source inspection only, including existing uncommitted work in both repositories. No application code, database records, or configuration changed. No tests, builds, migrations, browser automation, or external services were run. Database contents and applied migration status were not inspected.

**Recommendation:** extend the existing Vendure User, channel-scoped Role, TenantMembership, and tenant-policy architecture. Multi-workspace identity already exists. Customizable employee grants, protected business Owners, and secure employee invitations require new implementation. Do not introduce a separate credential store or parallel permission engine.

## Current architecture and reusable functionality

The [unified authentication implementation](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform-web/docs/UNIFIED_AUTHENTICATION_IMPLEMENTATION.md) is the current contract. [Account switching architecture](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform-web/docs/ACCOUNT_SWITCHING_AUTH_ARCHITECTURE.md) explicitly marks its older authentication descriptions as historical. General magic-link login and native public credential entry have been retired; purchase claims remain a separate purpose.

| Area | Implemented behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | One native Vendure `User` holds credentials. Customer and Administrator records can refer to that same User. A native Administrator record enables the Admin surface; it does not itself mean business Administrator or platform SuperAdmin. Verified personal phone and live Administrator checks participate in operational authentication. |
| Vendor ownership | `Vendor` links uniquely to a Seller and primary Channel. Human authority comes from `TenantMembership.principalId -> User`, with `vendorId`, role `owner` or `staff`, and status `invited`, `active`, or `revoked`. There is no protected single-owner pointer or unique-owner constraint. |
| Market ownership | `Market` has its own unique Channel. Its Channel uses a shared technical Seller, not the merchandise Vendor's Seller. Human memberships use the same TenantMembership table with `marketId` and role `marketAdmin`. Even `activateSignupOwner()` creates `marketAdmin`; Market Owner is not currently a distinct authorization concept. |
| Multiple memberships | Supported, including several Vendors, several Markets, and both types for one User. Uniqueness is per `(principalId, vendorId)` or `(principalId, marketId)`; each row targets exactly one business. Affiliation discovery returns active subjects with current exact-channel base grants. Self-service onboarding is unique per User/account type, so it is not a general additional-business creation workflow. |
| Membership versus grants | Membership and native Role assignment are separate requirements. Membership setters do not automatically provision matching native grants. Signup creates an Administrator on the existing User when needed, creates a Role on the new business Channel, and attaches that Role to the User. |
| Resource ownership | `OwnershipDirectory` resolves Vendor attribution and checks owned resources. Internal, readonly custom fields protect product/source ownership. `ProtectedOwnershipService` handles trusted provisioning assignments and rejects changing existing resource owners. This is resource attribution, not a human business-ownership transfer workflow. |
| Audit and events | DurableWorkService provides transactional audit records and outbox events. Vendor membership changes already emit audit/outbox facts; Market domain membership changes publish domain facts. Reuse these boundaries for team changes. |

### Roles, sessions, and workspace switching

Implemented membership labels are `owner`, `staff`, and `marketAdmin`. Native Vendure Roles contain permission arrays and Channel assignments; their union on the selected Channel supplies grants. Existing constants include a minimal Vendor Owner preset, Vendor Staff with only `ReadOwnVendorIdentity`, and a Market admin preset. Signup's Vendor preset additionally includes participation, catalog, and publication, but does not grant every business permission. Payment and POS integration owner presets are explicit opt-ins. Current Owner therefore does not automatically mean full business authority.

Central email/password login verifies native credentials and issues a central capability. Purpose-separated Shop/Admin handoffs are digest-backed, short-lived, single-use, correlation-bound, and origin-bound. Admin destination authorization reloads affiliations, binds the principal and selected Channel, and rechecks at exchange. Seven-day surface sessions do not freeze business membership authority. Shop-purpose sessions cannot authorize Admin APIs; selecting a URL/Channel token cannot grant membership.

Frontend reuse points are [AdminApp](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform-web/apps/admin/src/AdminApp.tsx), [AccountModes](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform-web/apps/admin/src/AccountModes.tsx), [account-login](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform-web/apps/admin/src/account-login.ts), [shared auth](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform-web/packages/auth/src/index.ts), and [admin-core](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform-web/packages/admin-core/src/index.ts). Session restoration selects a native user Channel; `resolveAdminContext()` validates business identity/membership and intersects backend current permissions with session-channel permissions. Navigation, `routeAllowed()`, and service adapters supplement backend enforcement. Vendor command guards require `owner`; Market context requires `marketAdmin`. Central [workspace selection](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform-web/apps/storefront/src/pages/account/workspaces.astro) and the Customer layout already render multiple server-authorized businesses. AdminApp clears services/context during refresh and rejects stale asynchronous results. There is no continuously pushed permission refresh in the inspected flow.

Backend session evidence: [account service](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/marketplace-commerce/customer-account/service.ts), [session policy](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/platform-identity/account-session-policy.ts).

## Existing enforcement and security concerns

[TenantAccessPolicy](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/platform-identity/tenant-access-policy.ts) already combines API type, authenticated principal, exact Channel, current database Role grants, current membership, and owned-resource checks. Memberships are reloaded on every invocation and locked for transactional calls. Vendor operations require an active Vendor; most capabilities additionally require `owner`. Exceptions include base reads, CRM, and explicitly granted POS checkout. Market policy requires `marketAdmin`; business-status checks are also enforced by relevant callers, including Market Operations, rather than uniformly inside `requireMarket()`.

Representative patterns inspected:

- [Vendor catalog](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/marketplace-commerce/vendor-catalog.service.ts) checks `catalog` and resource ownership inside transactions. [Operational inventory](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/marketplace-commerce/inventory/operational-inventory.service.ts) checks inventory or trusted POS authority, locks resources, validates Vendor order portions, and rechecks authority before mutation. Preserve these domain controls.
- [Commerce topology](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/marketplace-commerce/commerce/topology.api.ts) scopes Vendor order reads to owned portions and line links. However, those reads use the base `ReadOwnVendorIdentity`; Market occurrence order operations use `ReadOwnMarket`. Base workspace access is currently broader than independently selectable order-read access.
- [Market Operations](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/market-operations/service.ts) resolves an active Market from the Channel, requires current membership/base read, then requires the operation permission. Cross-workflow commands sometimes additionally require billing or occurrence grants. `own()` scopes resource IDs by Market.
- [Billing authority](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/billing-entitlements/authority.service.ts), [analytics authority](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/analytics/api.ts), and [communications authority](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/communications/consent.service.ts) have separate live checks. Changing TenantAccessPolicy alone will not migrate them all. Communications management retains Owner/marketAdmin role restrictions.

Principal limitations and risks:

- **No protected Owner invariant.** Vendor revocation checks the acting Owner but does not exclude an Owner target, self-revocation, or the last Owner. Trusted Vendor setters can change Owner roles/status; Market setters have no Owner distinction. Existing platform onboarding suspension can revoke an initial membership. These paths need explicit compatibility rules.
- **Role grants cannot alone delegate current workflows.** Giving Staff `ManageOwnCatalog` still fails the Owner role gate. Lead/Administrator roles are rejected by current database checks and frontend role validation. Market team roles are similarly unsupported.
- **Permissions are additive.** A shared Role across employees or Channels can spread an edit. A restrictive employee Role cannot subtract grants from another Role on the same Channel. Membership revocation currently does not remove native grants.
- **Immediate revocation is not universal.** Tenant-policy paths reload authority, but AccountSessionAccessStrategy primarily verifies session purpose/liveness and delegates ordinary permission evaluation to Vendure. Native resolvers do not automatically invoke TenantAccessPolicy. Vendure's installed session service clears caches for Role/Channel entity changes, but membership changes and join-table edits are not a sufficient universal cache-invalidation contract.
- **Generic API bypass risk is conditional, not a demonstrated exploit.** Native `ReadOrder`, `ReadCatalog`/`ReadProduct`, role/Administrator/Channel administration, and other broad grants can expose APIs outside tenant policy. Existing [customer privacy](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/platform-identity/customer-privacy.ts) and [native writer](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/marketplace-commerce/inventory/native-writer.guard.ts) interceptors block important native traversals/mutations, but do not establish a universal employee data boundary. Never grant generic native administration permissions to business Administrators.

`@Allow(Permission.Authenticated)` proves authentication, not business authority. Multiple permissions in `@Allow()` are alternatives, not a requirement to hold every grant; preserve service-level target checks and explicit additional permissions on compound commands.

Vendure `Permission.Owner` is an API resource/session ownership mechanism used by customer operations. It must not represent the proposed business Owner. `ManageVendorIdentity`, `ManageMarketIdentity`, `ManageBillingCatalog`, `ReadPlatformAnalytics`, and `SuperAdmin` remain trusted platform authority, never employee capabilities.

## Recommended business roles and permission categories

All behavior in this section is proposed. Keep one membership per User/business. Treat Staff, Lead, and Administrator as versioned permission suggestions copied when selected; the effective editable grant set remains native Vendure permissions. Preset changes should not silently rewrite existing employees.

| Role | Suggested starting authority |
| --- | --- |
| Staff | Operational reads plus selected POS/pickup tasks for Vendors; applications/assignments reads and check-in for Markets. No finance, refunds, team administration, or configuration by default. |
| Lead | Staff plus selected catalog/inventory or Market scheduling/application/assignment management. Optional refunds and financial reads require explicit Owner selection. |
| Administrator | Nearly all delegable business workflows. Routine team administration, if enabled, has a strict delegation ceiling. No protected ownership actions and no self-escalation. |
| Owner | Exactly one protected human authority per business, all applicable business capabilities and permission customization. Still subject to entitlements, provider readiness, validation, and domain safeguards. No platform provisioning authority. |

Reuse registered permissions below where their scope fits. Names in the final column describe additions or splits, not existing functionality. Current definitions are in [identity permissions](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/platform-identity/permissions.ts), [Market Operations permissions](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/market-operations/permissions.ts), and the specialist authority files above.

| Workflow category | Existing permission mechanisms | Required granularity decision/change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workspace/profile | Vendor `ReadOwnVendorIdentity`; Market `ReadOwnMarket` | Keep minimal discovery access separate from sensitive workflow reads. |
| Vendor catalog/publication | `ManageOwnCatalog`, `ManageCatalogPublication` | Delegate through tenant policy; separate read, pricing, tax settings, and publication if individually selectable. |
| Vendor inventory/orders/POS | `ManageOwnInventory`, `OperateOwnPos`; base identity reads for order portions | Split order read, fulfill/pickup, cancellation, inventory read/adjust. Inventory permission currently also covers fulfillment/cancellation. Preserve POS-specific internal scopes. |
| Vendor Market participation/applications | `ManageOwnMarketParticipation`; applicant authority currently requires Owner | Add delegated application read/submit/respond capabilities if intended; do not grant via base affiliation access. |
| Vendor customers/finance/refunds | `ReadOwnCRM`, `ReadOwnVendorFinance`, `RequestOwnVendorRefund`, `ManageOwnCoupon` | Keep customer projections/financial allocations scoped; consider separate coupon read/write and refund limits. |
| Vendor payment/integrations | `ReadOwnPaymentAccount`, `ManageOwnPaymentAccount`, `ReadOwnPosIntegrations`, `ManageOwnPosIntegrations` | Preserve Owner-only financial connection/ownership operations; split harmless integration configuration from protected account changes. |
| Market schedule/relationships/catalog | `ManageOwnMarketSchedule`, `ManageOwnMarketOccurrences`, `ManageOwnMarketMemberships`, `ManageOwnMarketListings`, `ManageOwnMarketOfferings` | Market memberships here mean Vendor participation relationships, not employees. Add distinct employee-team permissions. |
| Market applications/layouts/assignments | `ReadOwnMarketApplications` / `ManageOwnMarketApplications`, `ReadOwnMarketLayouts` / `ManageOwnMarketLayouts`, `ReadOwnMarketAssignments` / `ManageOwnMarketAssignments` | Reuse pairs; applications currently also cover directory/rental catalog. Split only where product requires independent access. |
| Market day/booth billing | `OperateOwnMarketDay`, `ReadOwnMarketBilling`, `ManageOwnMarketBilling` | Separate day close from check-in if needed; retain additional billing checks on compound actions. |
| Storefront | Vendor `ManageOwnStorefront`; Market `ManageMarketStorefront` | Decide edit versus publish; retain domain/routing and protected identity boundaries. |
| Subscription/analytics/communications | `ReadOwnBilling`, `ManageOwnBilling`; `ReadOwnVendorAnalytics`, `ReadOwnMarketAnalytics`; `ReadOwnCommunications`, `ManageOwnMarketing` | Keep subscription billing distinct from booth billing and payment ownership; retain entitlement and consent checks. |
| Team/protected operations | `ManageOwnVendorMembership` currently supports Owner revocation/list visibility; Market employee setters are platform-only | Add scoped team read/invite/revoke/grant-edit commands. Ownership transfer/deletion and financial ownership require explicit Owner checks regardless of editable grants. |

Use a dedicated native Role per membership, restricted to that business Channel and linked from the membership. Store preset metadata separately from native effective grants; do not also maintain an independent JSON permission truth. Enforce an allowlist of business permissions, reject other tenant Roles/grants that could override customization, and update membership/grants/audit atomically. Extend TenantAccessPolicy and specialist authorities to authorize capabilities instead of blanket role labels, retaining an explicit protected-Owner check.

For immediate effect, every employee data read and mutation must consult current membership and effective exact-channel authority. Extend the existing session/access strategy for tenant requests and close native API gaps; retain service-level resource checks. Cache only within a request, invalidate native session permission caches after grant changes, and serialize revocation/grant edits with protected mutations. Define completion at transaction commit: no newly authorized work after revocation commits; already-running work must serialize or revalidate before committing. Workers and provider callbacks need explicit service authority or reauthorization, not copied employee-session grants.

## Invitation and membership lifecycle

**Implemented infrastructure:** optional signed-in mailbox proof (`VERIFY_ACCOUNT`) records `emailVerifiedAt` and the exact `emailVerifiedAddress`; phone signup does not require general email activation. Account capabilities provide random tokens, digest storage, expiry, database-clock validation, locking, single-use consumption, and durable rate limits. [Account delivery configuration](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/vendure-config.ts) supplies `sendAccountEmail`; [ResendAccountEmailSender](E:/coding/farmers-market-platform/src/plugins/communications/account-email-sender.ts) enforces configured network approval, sender constraints, suppression checks, and delivery idempotency. Local file delivery exists. EmailPlugin has no native account handlers configured. Purchase/POS invitations and application business-link requests do not grant employee membership.

**New lifecycle required:**

1. An authorized inviter selects a business, normalized email, preset, and allowed capabilities. Persist a separate invitation because TenantMembership requires an existing principal. Record business, inviter, proposed grants/version, token digest, expiry, and acceptance/revocation timestamps; enforce one pending invitation per business/email. Rate-limit creation, resend, and redemption. Use durable delivery with controlled retries; keep raw secrets out of ordinary outbox/audit metadata and logs.
2. Send a purpose-specific invitation link to the central account origin. Opening GET only displays intent and moves the token out of the visible URL into protected continuation state. It does not authenticate, consume, or activate membership. Use private/no-store responses, no-referrer policy, and CSRF/origin protection for acceptance POSTs. Resend rotates the token; cancel invalidates it. Expired/revoked invitations grant nothing.
3. Require canonical login or ordinary same-User signup with existing phone requirements. Retain the invitation through that flow without starting business provisioning. Do not create a second identity, attach a business by email alone, or repurpose purchase/recovery/handoff capabilities.
4. Acceptance requires both the authenticated intended identity and mailbox proof for the exact invited current email. The emailed invitation can supply that narrowly scoped proof when securely bound and consumed, or use the existing signed-in email-proof flow. Decide whether acceptance also records general mailbox verification. An email match or phone proof alone is insufficient. Unverified/conflicting legacy identities require the existing proof/recovery or trusted remediation policy, never automatic merging or credential replacement.
5. In one locked transaction, recheck invitation state/expiry, principal/email, business status, inviter's current delegation authority, and proposed grant validity. Create/reuse the same User's Administrator record, activate the unique membership, assign its Channel Role, consume the invitation, and emit audit/events. Concurrent acceptance must be idempotent without resurrecting revoked membership or accepting superseded grants.
6. Revocation removes workspace access and its managed native grants, cancels pending invitations as appropriate, and refreshes authority. Preserve the identity, Customer history, other memberships, and audit history. Rejoining requires explicit authorization and fresh acceptance.

## Protected ownership safeguards

Create a protected Owner principal reference for each Vendor and Market, backed by a valid active Owner membership and a uniqueness constraint preventing multiple active Owners. Only a dedicated transaction may change this relationship. Ordinary membership/grant APIs must reject Owner removal, demotion, self-removal, or assigning Owner through a preset. Lock the business and affected memberships so concurrent requests cannot orphan ownership.

Owner-only operations must compare the live principal to that protected reference in the domain service, not rely on a Role name, editable permission, client flag, or generic native `Permission.Owner`. Define a non-delegable protected set covering transfer, deletion, legal/payee/bank ownership and protected integration connect/disconnect operations. Reject protected grants in invitation and grant-edit inputs. Administrators cannot edit their own authority or route changes through peers to exceed the Owner-approved delegation ceiling. Owner credential recovery and any platform emergency override need explicit policy and audit.

If transfer is introduced, require recent reauthentication, target acceptance/proof, transactional Owner replacement, grant/cache updates, and audit. Do not expose a generic owner field update. Existing resource-owner reassignment protections must remain independent.

## Further authorization review and product decisions

Implementation needs targeted review of native/nested GraphQL reads, bulk/export/media/settings APIs, Shop applicant flows, separate billing/analytics/communications authorities, notification recipients, documents, and trusted external-commerce/POS/worker scopes. Verify new employee access does not inherit sensitive projections through base read grants, and that revocation prevents queued employee-initiated work where required. This audit sampled representative Vendor catalog/orders/inventory and Market operations; it is not exhaustive endpoint certification.

Product clarification is required for:

- Who becomes Owner for existing Markets and businesses with multiple/no apparent Owners; whether platform emergency recovery is permitted.
- Exact Staff/Lead defaults, independent read/write/pricing/refund limits, and which financial/integration details remain protected.
- Whether Administrators can invite/revoke only lower roles, modify others' grants, or invite peers; Owner-only grant customization is the simplest initial policy.
- Whether employees may apply to Markets, publish storefronts, close a Market day, export customer data, or manage subscriptions.
- Invitation lifetime, resend/cancel/rejoin rules, acceptance after inviter revocation, and whether invitation proof also verifies the general account email.
- Ownership transfer/deletion scope, business suspension access, and treatment of already-running/queued work when permissions change.
