# 4. Themes

> **READ-ONLY REFERENCE.** Do not edit this file during feature work. If the code disagrees with it, stop and ask the developer. Change it only when the developer explicitly asks. Verified against `farmers-market-platform-web` (working tree based on `fb09982`) and `farmers-market-platform` (working tree based on `ea2c77a`), November 27, 2026.

**Layer: Structure + Styling.** What each vendor theme does to meet the standard. Vendor themes do not use the market theme folder contract ([Structure contract](../market-storefronts/03_STRUCTURE_CONTRACT.md)); each keeps its existing shell. The standard is met inside those shells.

## Every vendor theme

- **Header:** builds its links, then drops any page in `themePageStructure(theme, 'VENDOR').footerOnlyPages`. It never hard-codes FAQ, Policies or Terms into the header.
- **Header cart:** shows the item count (`MarketCartLink`).
- **Footer:** the market footer's columns, **Site** and **About** (see [Pages](02_PAGES.md#header-and-footer-links)). Terms always come through `VendorTermsLink`.
- **Policies:** no closing invitation (`noClosingPages`), wherever the theme draws the page.
- **Shop (`/products`):** the shared `HarvestShop`, with the shared `NumberedPagination` when there is more than one page. A theme with its own shop heading renders its hero above the shop and passes `themeHero`.
- **Product page:** the shared market product page without the vendor card (`pages/products/[slug].astro`).
- **Product cards:** nothing about availability while the product can be added. A low-stock warning ("N left in stock") or a short note next to a disabled button otherwise.
- **Checkout:** `VendorCheckout` with the staged layout. **Contact:** the shared contact page in the market form layout.
- **Measurements (shared CSS):** the "Vendor storefront standard" blocks scoped to `.vendor-site` give every vendor theme the market standard's measurements: `vendor.css` (the `--vs-*` tokens: content column, edge, soft tint, shade, filter icons), `vendor-shop.css` (shop column and grid, category pills, cards, product page), `vendor-cart.css` (cart groups), `vendor-checkout.css` (step chips, stage cards, pickup choices, summary, checkout column) and `contact.css` (two-column form). Colors come from each theme's `--brand-*` tokens. The selectors start `.vendor-site .vendor-shop …` so the standard's sizes win over a theme's older shop rules; a theme still decorates (Bloom keeps its image shapes).
- **Section renderers:** Image with Text writes `data-image-shape` on the section and `data-hero-shape` on the image or placeholder for a side image (the shared shape rules in `packages/theme/src/storefront.css` draw it). Call-to-Action Banner writes `data-button-style` and uses the chosen style for its button.
- **Section spacing:** `SiteLayout` adds `vendor-site` to every vendor website's wrapper. `styles/website-sections.css` gives `.vendor-site :is(section.website-section, section.harvest-library)` `margin-block: var(--website-space-block, 32px)`. Sections sit in block flow, so where two meet the larger margin wins. A theme must not put library sections in a flex or grid container that stops margins collapsing.

## Harvest and Bloom

Shared Harvest vendor shell: `home/HarvestHeader.astro`, `home/HarvestFooter.astro`, `home/HarvestShop.astro`, the Harvest section renderers in `components/home/`, and the shared pages. Bloom adds only `styles/bloom.css`. The header and footer are vendor-only; market Harvest has its own in `components/market-harvest/`.

- Header: FAQ removed by the rule. Order: Shop, Contact, Find Us, About.
- Already met before the standard: image shape and button style in the Harvest renderers, `ProductCards` availability, Policies via `PolicyListPage`.

## Editorial Market

Own shell: `editorial-market/ShopHeader.astro` (the header on every page, shop and product included), `Footer.astro`, `Home.astro`, `ShopHero.astro`, `Content.astro` (About, FAQ, Policies) and its own Text, Image with Text and Call-to-Action Banner renderers, styled by `editorial-market.css`. All of them are vendor-only now: market Editorial has its own header, footer, homepage and `market-editorial.css`.

- The header drops footer-only pages through the rule and shows the cart count (`.em-cart-link`).
- `Content.astro`: Policies leaves out its closing invitation by the rule. Policies opens with the theme's `em-content-hero` heading because no page hero is derived. Market websites no longer use `Content.astro`; they use the shared About, FAQ and Policies pages.
- Shop: `ShopHero.astro` (the editable shop heading over the Editorial artwork) above the shared `HarvestShop`. `Shop.astro`, `Product.astro` and their helpers (`ShopCategories`, `ProductInformationTabs`, `ProductMarketSelector`) are no longer routed. `Header.astro` was already unused.
- `ImageWithText.astro`, `CtaBanner.astro`: image shape and button style. Until a style is chosen, the banner button keeps Editorial's filled look. A shaped image's frame drops its paper backing (`.em-image-story[data-image-shape] .em-story-image`), so nothing shows around the shape.
- Known, unchanged: Editorial's `.em-page` column is `min(100% - 2rem, 76rem)` wide, so the Find Us page sits 1rem from the edge on narrow windows, as other Editorial content pages do.

## Modern Farmhouse

- **Premium** (template version 2): `modern-farmhouse/Header.astro` already leaves FAQ and Policies out of the header and shows the bag's item count (`.farmhouse-cart-link`). `Footer.astro` has the Site and About columns. The shop is `ShopHero.astro` above the shared `HarvestShop`; the product page and checkout are the shared ones. `Shop.astro`, `ProductLayout.astro`, `Gallery.astro`, `ProductInformation.astro`, `Purchase.tsx` and the `Checkout` component in `Checkout.tsx` are no longer routed (`Checkout.tsx` still supplies the confirmation page's `Summary`). The cart keeps `Cart.tsx`. `Content.astro` leaves out the Policies invitation by the rule. Farmhouse's own `ProductCards.astro` now draws only the homepage carousel (`FeaturedCarousel`); shop and product cards are the shared `ProductCards`. `Photo.astro` takes an optional `shape` for Image with Text, and `farmhouse-v2.css` lets a shaped photo size itself (`.farmhouse-editorial[data-image-shape]`), because the frame's fixed height would otherwise turn a circle into an ellipse; `CtaBanner.astro` honours the button style. `FindUs.astro` takes `fixed` (see [Find Us](03_FIND_US.md)).
- **Legacy** (`mf-` shell, no premium template): `legacy-modern-farmhouse/Header.astro` drops FAQ from the mobile menu by the rule. Its pages are the shared ones.

## New components and why

From the vendor storefront alignment (November 27, 2026):

- `FeaturedProducts.astro`: the homepage's featured-products block for each theme (Harvest featured section, Editorial Product Showcase, Farmhouse carousel). It was inline markup in each homepage.
- `StepperGlyph.tsx`: the plus and minus marks in every quantity stepper, drawn with CSS bars so they are centred whatever the font. The shared icon sets have a plus but no matching minus.
- `modern-farmhouse/ShopHero.astro`: Farmhouse's shop heading over the hero photo, extracted from `Shop.astro` so it can sit above the shared `HarvestShop`.
- Extended, not new: `MarketCartLink` takes a theme's own cart drawing as children with a `className`; `Checkout` takes `stagedLayout`; `HarvestShop` takes `themeHero`; `ProductPurchase` always uses the cart's quantity stepper and shows only the low-stock line; `FeaturedAdd` and `ProductPurchase` animate on vendor websites too; `ContactForm` lays out the vendor fields in the market form grid.

Earlier: no new component was created. Show more markets is an optional `paging` prop on the existing `MarketSchedule`, using the shared `Button`. Numbered paging reuses `NumberedPagination`. Farmhouse image shapes reuse the shared `data-hero-shape` rules through an optional prop on the existing `Photo`.

## Adding a vendor theme

1. Add its ID to `vendorStandardThemes` in `packages/theme/src/theme-page-structure.ts`.
2. Build its header and footer to the link rules above, reading `footerOnlyPages`.
3. If it draws its own shop, product cards, content pages, Find Us or section renderers, apply the matching rule from the "Every vendor theme" list to each.
4. Keep its library sections in block flow so `.vendor-site` spacing works.
5. Add it to `tests/vendor-standard.test.ts` and check it with [Testing and preview](05_TESTING_AND_PREVIEW.md).
