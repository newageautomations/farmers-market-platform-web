**Layer: Process.** The market [Testing and preview](../market-storefronts/10_TESTING_AND_PREVIEW.md) document applies in full: the persistent test database `vendure_demo_market_ops` is never reset, dropped, truncated, reseeded or replaced, and the environment starts only through the four `demo:market-operations:*` scripts. ## Previewing without changing the test data

- **Another theme on a real host:** a temporary, development-only check in `middleware.ts` that swaps `appearance.theme` from a cookie. Premium Modern Farmhouse also needs `template: { id: 'modern-farmhouse', version: 2, settings: farmhouseDefaults() }`. Use a Harvest-content vendor such as Showbread Catering for Farmhouse and Editorial: Bloom's saved content does not render under those shells.
- **Show more markets:** a temporary, development-only block in `pages/find-us.astro` that repeats the loaded appearances (or one sample appearance) to 6 or more rows.
- Remove both before finishing, and clear the cookies.

## Automated checks (web)

- `tests/vendor-standard.test.ts`: every vendor theme gets the vendor rules, market Harvest and Editorial stay historical, Find Us is fixed and saved sections are kept.
- `tests/website-sections.test.tsx`: page heroes per kind (Policies and Find Us have none on a vendor website).
- `tests/pasture.test.ts`, `tests/meadow.test.ts`, `tests/hearth.test.ts`: the market standard is unchanged.
- `npm run typecheck --workspace @market/storefront` and `npx tsc --noEmit` (filter to `^(apps|packages)/`).

## Browser checks that matter

For each vendor theme:

1. The header has no FAQ, Policies or Terms link, on desktop and in the mobile menu. The footer has them when on.
2. Contact and FAQ open with a 220px page hero. Policies and Terms open with their own heading and have no closing invitation.
3. Find Us: one hidden `h1`, no hero, introduction, sections or closing. With 6 or more appearances: 4 rows, then 12, then all; the button disappears at the end; focus lands on the first new row; empty months are hidden.
4. Library sections have 32px above and below, and the larger margin wins where two meet.
5. A market website on Harvest or Editorial Market has no `vendor-site` class and its pages are unchanged.
