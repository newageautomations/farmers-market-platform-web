## Previewing without changing the test data

- **Another theme on a real host:** a temporary, development-only check in `middleware.ts` that swaps `appearance.theme` from a cookie. Premium Modern Farmhouse also needs `template: { id: 'modern-farmhouse', version: 2, settings: farmhouseDefaults() }` and Editorial Market `template: { id: 'editorial-market', version: 1, settings: editorialMarketStarterSettings() }`; for both, drop `harvestComposition` and `pageSections` from the swapped appearance, which their contracts reject.
- **Cart and checkout:** a temporary dev-only page that renders `VendorCheckout` (vendor) or `Checkout` with `marketLayout` (market) with a mock `CheckoutView`, answering `/api/shop` by `action` (`checkout-pickup` → pickup options, `checkout-contact` → `null`, anything else → the view), reaches Contact, Pickup and Review without writing to the test data.
- **Show more markets:** a temporary, development-only block in `pages/find-us.astro` that repeats the loaded appearances (or one sample appearance) to 6 or more rows.
- Remove both before finishing, and clear the cookies.

## Automated checks (web)

- `tests/vendor-standard.test.ts`: every vendor theme gets the vendor rules, market Harvest and Editorial follow the market standard (with booth cards off) while their vendor namesakes keep the vendor rules, Find Us is fixed and saved sections are kept.
- `tests/animated-cart-button.test.tsx`, `tests/product-details.test.tsx`: vendor add buttons animate without the text message; the product page shows only the low-stock line.
- `tests/editorial-market.test.tsx`, `tests/website-sections.test.tsx`: the Editorial vendor homepage's default order and closing banner.
- `tests/website-sections.test.tsx`: page heroes per kind (Policies and Find Us have none on a vendor website).
- `tests/pasture.test.ts`, `tests/meadow.test.ts`, `tests/hearth.test.ts`: the market standard is unchanged.
- `npm run typecheck --workspace @market/storefront` and `npx tsc --noEmit` (filter to `^(apps|packages)/`).

## Browser checks that matter

For each vendor theme:

1. The header has no FAQ, Policies or Terms link, on desktop and in the mobile menu. The footer has them when on.
2. Contact and FAQ open with a 220px page hero. Policies and Terms open with their own heading and have no closing invitation.
3. Find Us: one hidden `h1`, no hero, introduction, sections or closing. With 6 or more appearances: 4 rows, then 12, then all; the button disappears at the end; focus lands on the first new row; empty months are hidden.
4. Library sections have 32px above and below, and the larger margin wins where two meet.
5. A market website on Harvest or Editorial Market has no `vendor-site` class and uses `.market-harvest-site` or `.market-editorial-site`.
6. At 1440px the shop matches the market standard: a 1220px column, a 230px side panel, 223px-wide cards with `1.1rem` names and `1.15rem` prices, 40px card buttons. The product page has no vendor card and the cart's quantity stepper.
7. Add to cart animates on the button and shows no "Added to your cart" text; the header cart count updates.
8. Checkout shows numbered step chips; on Pickup, choices lift on hover (check with a real hover and `getComputedStyle`).
9. The footer has Site and About columns.
